# Welcome to Ymer

This workspace starts with a few pages to show you around. Change them, move them, or delete them: they are yours.

## Write together

This page is a **document**. Everyone with access edits it at the same time, and you see their cursors.

- Type **@** to link to another page, like [Tasks](Tasks.csv). Linked pages list this page under *Backlinks*.
- Select text to comment on it, or to ask the AI to rewrite it.
- Paste or drop an image to add it.
- Open *History* to see or restore an earlier version.

## Organise with databases

[Tasks](Tasks.csv) is a **database**: a table of rows with typed columns. Switch to the *Board* view to drag tasks between statuses, or add a *Chart* view.

## Think visually

[Brainstorm](Brainstorm.mindmap.json) is a **mind map**. Select an idea and press Tab to add one under it. There are also **drawings** for sketches and diagrams.

## Bring your own content

- Use **Templates** in the sidebar for meeting notes, project briefs, trackers and more.
- Use **Import** to bring in Markdown files, or a whole Notion workspace (export it as *Markdown & CSV*).
- Turn any page into a template of your own with **Save as template** in its menu.
